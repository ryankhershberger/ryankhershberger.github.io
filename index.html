1-100 Blitz

Tap 1 to 100. Beat the clock.

Download on the App Store

How to play

  • Tap the numbers in order, starting from 1.
  • They drift around the screen and bounce off each other.
  • Three wrong taps ends the run.
  • Beat your best time and climb the Game Center leaderboards.
